
#Designed a 5-stage current-starved ring oscillator for a frequency range of 30 MHz to 70 MHz with a gain of 60 MHz/V to 90 MHz/V. 


#Each stage had output parasitic capacitance of 10 fF, and the oscillator had almost rail-to-rail output signal swing. Current mirrors were used to provide a constant current through each stage, which was provided by a mosfet controlled by a control voltage. 


#Simulated the circuit to analyze the output voltage and also the frequency vs control voltage plot to check the gain and tuned the circuit accordingly.
